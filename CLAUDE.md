## Circuit and electrical verdict

- **Circuit:** a 555 astable blinker.
  - **Timing:** R2 (1k) from supply to DIS, R1 (10k) from DIS to THR/TR, C1 (100 µF) on THR. That's about 0.76 s high and 0.69 s low (≈ 0.7 Hz).
  - **Output:** the `LED` net is the 555 output (pin 3), **not ground**. D1–D3 go +6V → LED → 270 Ω → output and light while the output is low. D4–D6 go output → 270 Ω → LED → GND and light while it's high. The two groups alternate.
  - **Power:** the battery's + terminal goes through SW1 to the `+6V` net. **The name is misleading: the supply is one CR2032, i.e. 3 V.**
- **As designed, it won't work properly.** The **LM555 is bipolar**: it needs ≥ 4.5 V, and its output high level only reaches about Vcc − 1.7 V, so D4–D6 would never light.
- **Required fix (no footprint change):** use a **CMOS 555** in DIP-8.
  - Chosen low-cost part: **XINLUDA XD7555** (an ICM7555 clone; 2–18 V, about 60 µA idle).
  - Alternatives: TLC555CP, ICM7555IPA, LMC555CN.
  - In KiCad, change U1's symbol to `Timer:ICM7555xP` (or `TLC555xP`). Pinout is identical, so the PCB doesn't change. **This symbol swap has NOT been done yet** (the schematic still says `Timer:LM555xN`).
- **LED colours:** use **red or yellow** (forward voltage about 1.8–2.0 V). Blue and white (about 3 V) will barely light from a coin cell.
- **Caveat:** CMOS 555s source current weakly, so D4–D6 will be dimmer than D1–D3.
- **Optional, no footprint change:**
  - Use **R1 = 100k, R2 = 10k, C1 = 10 µF** instead of 10k / 1k / 100 µF. Timing is the same, and it wastes about 0.3 mA instead of about 3 mA through R2.
  - Lower the 270 Ω resistors to about 150 Ω for brighter LEDs.
- **There is no decoupling capacitor footprint.** It will very likely work without one. If a board glitches, bodge a 100 nF capacitor across U1 pins 8 and 1.
- **Power budget estimate:** about 10–12 mA average (3 LEDs at about 3 mA each, plus R2 discharge, plus about 0.1 mA for the 555). A CR2032 at that drain gives roughly **10–15 h** of blinking, dimming as the cell drains.

## Gotchas

- **KiCad overwrites files it has open.** Check for `~badges.kicad_pcb.lck` or a running `kicad` process before editing files directly; close KiCad first.
- **Generating Gerbers:**
  - Press **B** to refill the zones and save first; Gerbers are plotted from the saved zone fill.
  - Also generate the drill file: Gerbers alone carry no holes.
- **KiCad auto-backup** was turned off globally on the original Mac (`~/Library/Preferences/kicad/10.0/kicad_common.json`, `auto_backup.enabled=false`).
- `.gitignore` should contain `~*.lck`, `*-backups/` and `*.kicad_prl`. Check that it does; the current file may only list `.DS_Store`.
- **Tools on the original machine:**
  - KiCad CLI: `/Applications/KiCad/KiCad.app/Contents/MacOS/kicad-cli`
  - Python with `pcbnew`: `/Applications/KiCad/KiCad.app/Contents/Frameworks/Python.framework/Versions/3.9/bin/python3`
